🗺️ Proof of Walk cards

Your statue, your rank, your quote.

Every statue you walk to is a card in your passport, and the check-in that earns a rank becomes that rank’s card. The front shows the Satoshi statue you walked to, your rank and a GPS-verified seal with the date. The back carries one of 21 Bitcoin quotes, the details of your check-in and a code anyone can scan to verify it.

How it works

  1. Rank 1

    👣 Confirmed ✓

    Your first GPS-verified card at any Satoshi statue. Your card shows that statue.

  2. Rank 2

    🧱 Genesis Block ✓

    Collect cards from any 3 of the original 10 statues. Your card shows the statue that completed it.

  3. Rank 3

    🏛️ Grand High Order ✓

    The Genesis Block plus 1 more statue, anywhere on the map. Your card shows the statue that got you there.

Every GPS-verified check-in is a card in your passport, one per statue: that statue, your rank at the time, a quote and its own verify code. The check-in that earns a rank is that rank’s card, and each rank is earned at a different statue. Once you have a card, it’s yours and it never changes.

Rarity shows how often a design is drawn. It isn't a measure of value, and cards can't be bought, sold or traded here.

The Legendary Genesis card

Card No. 21 · Legendary

About 1 in 121 cards is the Legendary Genesis card, at any statue and any rank.

Cards are just for fun: free, and not linked to tips or the giveaway.

Its quote is the newspaper headline Satoshi Nakamoto wrote into Bitcoin’s very first block, block 0, on January 3, 2009:

The Times 03/Jan/2009 Chancellor on brink of second bailout for banks

It shows when Bitcoin started and why: a new kind of money, launched while banks were being bailed out. Those words are still in block 0 today, and every Bitcoin block since builds on it.

Verify a card

Every card has a verify code like PW-7K3Q-9QX2 and a QR code. Scan it, or type it on the verify page: it shows only the statue, the date and the rank. No name, no exact time, no location.

Show yours off

Right after a check-in that earns a rank, tap Share under your card to post it, or save the image. The link you share goes to a page for that statue and rank, for example Genesis Block at Lugano, Genesis Block at El Zonte, Genesis Block at Tokyo.

You can also download print-ready files of your card (front and back) from your card on the Proof of Walk page. Ordering a printed card is coming soon.
