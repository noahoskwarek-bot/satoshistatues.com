🃏 Proof of Walk cards

Walk to Satoshi statues, earn ranks, collect cards.

Every rank you earn comes with a collectible card. There are 21 designs, and each one carries a famous Bitcoin saying. Which design you get is drawn when you earn the rank. Once you have a card, it’s yours and it never changes.

Earn a rank, get a card

Check in at a statue (within 150 m) to collect a GPS-verified stamp. There are 3 ranks, and each gives you a card.

  1. Rank 1

    👣 Confirmed

    Your first GPS-verified stamp at any Satoshi statue.

  2. Rank 2

    🧱 Genesis Block

    Stamp any 3 of the original 10 statues.

  3. Rank 3

    🏛️ Grand High Order of Fellow Travelers

    The Genesis Block plus 1 more statue, anywhere on the map.

Your card keeps the look of your rank, then adds its own foil frame, serial number (like 07 / 21) and saying.

Odds are per card drawn, out of 121. The diamonds under the serial number show the tier: one for Common, up to five for 1 of 121.

The 1 of 121 Genesis card

Card No. 21 · the rarest design

Each time you earn a rank, at any statue, your card gets its own draw, with a 1 in 121 chance of being the Genesis card.

That draw is separate from everything else: like the ranks, cards are just for fun, free, and not linked to tips or the giveaway.

Its saying is the newspaper headline Satoshi Nakamoto wrote into Bitcoin’s very first block, block 0, on January 3, 2009:

The Times 03/Jan/2009 Chancellor on brink of second bailout for banks

It shows when Bitcoin started and why: a new kind of money, launched while banks were being bailed out. Those words are still in block 0 today, and every Bitcoin block since builds on it.
