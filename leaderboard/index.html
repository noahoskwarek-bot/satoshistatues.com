How it works

Open a statue on the map and tap Check in here when you are within 150 m. The server checks the distance and records the stamp; your location itself is not stored. Each device can stamp each statue once, up to 5 new stamps in 24 hours. People are ranked by unique GPS-verified stamps; on a tie, whoever got there first ranks higher.

GPS can be faked, so treat this as a friendly game.
