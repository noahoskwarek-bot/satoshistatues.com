About

Chrome/fiberglass robotic bull with blue laser eyes, commissioned by TradeStation and designed by Furio Tedeschi. Debuted at the Bitcoin 2022 conference in Miami Beach, then moved to its permanent home at Miami Dade College. A modern take on the Wall Street bull.

📖 The story

Artist
Designed by Furio Tedeschi, a Paramount Pictures concept artist (Transformers 5, Bumblebee); fabricated by Onyx Forge.Sources: MDC The Reporter (22 Apr 2022)
Unveiled
Debuted at the Bitcoin 2022 conference in Miami Beach (6–9 Apr 2022). Miami Dade College held its ribbon-cutting on 14 Apr 2022.Sources: MDC The Reporter (22 Apr 2022)
Commissioned by
TradeStation. It was unveiled by Mayor Francis Suarez.Sources: MDC The Reporter (22 Apr 2022) TradeStation: Miami Bull
What the design means
A futuristic take on Wall Street's Charging Bull, "a prominent community symbol of modern finance". It was placed at Miami Dade College to tie in with TradeStation's financial-literacy partnership. The college's president said: "The bull represents the future and we represent the future."Sources: TradeStation: Miami Bull MDC The Reporter (22 Apr 2022)
Materials
Chrome and fiberglass, about 3,000 lb and 11 ft tall. MDC The Reporter cites a Forbes estimate of more than $250,000 in cost.Sources: MDC The Reporter (22 Apr 2022)

Only facts we could source. Items tagged “Reported” are not fully confirmed.

🧭 Visiting

Getting there

Per OpenStreetMap, the Metromover's College North station is about 90 m away, and College Bayside about 200 m. A Miami-Dade Transit bus stop at NE 1st Avenue and NE 4th Street is about 55 m away. Metrorail Government Center (about 490 m) and Brightline MiamiCentral (about 400 m) are also close. Routes and schedules are unverified.

Parking: OpenStreetMap shows Miami Parking Authority Garage 3, paid, about 200 m away, plus Miami Dade College garages nearby whose public access is unverified.

Exact spot

At the edge of Miami Dade College's Wolfson Campus in front of Building 1000, with its eyes facing NE 1st Avenue (MDC The Reporter). The site data puts it at NE 1st Ave and NE 4th St. Pin: 25.77820, -80.19186 (OpenStreetMap node 10677295005).

Photo tip

This isn't a see-through design. It's an 11-foot chrome-and-fiberglass bull with blue "laser" eyes.

Generic tips (not from a source): shoot low and slightly to one side so the horns and eyes stand out against the sky, and try dusk if the eyes are lit (unverified).

Hours & access

Outdoors on an open campus. MDC The Reporter described visitors walking up for selfies, and TradeStation calls MDC the bull's permanent home. We found no posted hours, so viewing hours are unverified.
