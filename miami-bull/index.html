About

Chrome/fiberglass robotic bull with blue laser eyes, commissioned by TradeStation and designed by Furio Tedeschi. Debuted at the Bitcoin 2022 conference in Miami Beach, then moved to its permanent home at Miami Dade College. A modern take on the Wall Street bull.
