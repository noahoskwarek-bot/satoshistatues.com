Artist

25 Apr 2025, at the grand opening of Tokyo Bitcoin Base (about 100 guests).Sources: Forbes (8 Sep 2025)
Commissioned by
Reported Tokyo Bitcoin Base, which was opened by Fulgur Ventures. Who bought the statue has not been stated.Sources: Forbes (8 Sep 2025)
What the design means
Seated hacker with a laptop on his lap, built from 61 thin mirror-polished stainless-steel profiles with gaps between them (a technique taken from sculptor Julian Voss-Andreae), on a Corten-steel base. It is clear from the side and almost invisible head-on, standing for Satoshi's anonymity and retreat from public life. Someone standing behind it is reflected in the face, so "We are all Satoshi". The "cliché" hacker deliberately honours all the developers who built Bitcoin. It was unveiled alongside the Base's mission to "make Japan a great Bitcoin nation again".Sources: Bitcoin Austria: Vienna unveiling Forbes (8 Sep 2025)

🧭 Visiting

Getting there

Tokyo Bitcoin Base lists its access as a 6-minute walk from Yotsuya Station, and a 9-minute walk from both Ichigaya Station and Akebonobashi Station. That's from the venue's website as archived in Nov 2025. The live site showed a maintenance notice on 2026-10-21.

Per OpenStreetMap, the nearest Yotsuya Station entrance is about 320 m away, and Akebonobashi (Toei Subway, S03) about 640 m. Parking is unverified.

Exact spot

Outside Tokyo Bitcoin Base, 2-8 Yotsuya-Honshiocho, Shinjuku-ku, Tokyo 160-0003 (Forbes: the statue "sits outside of Tokyo Bitcoin Base"). Forbes describes the building as four stories on a relatively quiet block. Pin: 35.68897, 139.72854 (OpenStreetMap node for Tokyo Bitcoin Base).

Photo tip

It's a Satoshigallery design, so the figure is visible from the side and nearly disappears from the front. Take both angles. The exact best viewpoint at this site is unverified.

Generic tip (not from a source): it's a street-side spot, so go early or late to avoid passers-by in the frame.

Hours & access

The statue is outdoors outside the building. Opening hours for viewing it are unverified. Inside, the co-working space offers drop-ins on weekdays from 10:00 to 18:00 for ¥5,500 per day, with Lightning accepted, per the archived Nov 2025 site.
