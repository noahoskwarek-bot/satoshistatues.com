About

Unveiled on 11 Nov 2025 in Miami Beach, brought to Miami by Cantor Fitzgerald’s Brandon and Kyle Lutnick; Michael Saylor attended and helped unveil it. A permanent waterfront park location was announced but has not been named, so the map pin is approximate.

Not yet installed, so it can’t be stamped in the Satoshi Passport (not counted in the total).

What the design means
A metal hacker sitting cross-legged with a laptop that "aims to give the viewer a sense of disappearance… when the observer places themselves behind the statue, they become Satoshi".Sources: Miami New Times (12 Nov 2025)
