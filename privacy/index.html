Privacy

Short version: no accounts, no locations kept, and IP addresses only as hashes for 7 days.

Satoshi Passport and leaderboard

  • Your passport stamps are saved in your browser (localStorage).
  • When you check in, your browser sends your location, a random device ID (created in your browser, not linked to your name) and the statue to our leaderboard server. The server only uses the location to check that you are within 150 m. Coordinates are not stored. We keep the statue, the time and a rough distance band such as “under 50 m”.
  • If you add a nickname or X handle, it is shown publicly on the leaderboard and in the recent check-ins feed.
  • The first time you use the leaderboard, stamps already in your passport are added to it.
  • Proof of Walk ranks are worked out from your GPS-verified stamps. If you stamp 10 or more statues, your nickname (or “Anonymous #…”) and the date are listed on the Fellow Travelers page.
  • If you add a Lightning address to your passport, it is used only to recover your passport on a new device, together with a one-time recovery code we show you once. We keep a hashed copy of the address and of the code plus a masked form for you to see, never the address itself. It is never shown publicly, never paid and never contacted. Recovery attempts are counted per network (as a salted hash, kept 1 day). Clear the field and save to delete it and switch recovery off.
  • IP addresses are only used for rate limiting. They are stored as a salted, per-day hash and deleted after 7 days.
  • The server runs on Cloudflare Workers with a Cloudflare D1 database.

Analytics

We use GoatCounter for privacy-friendly page counts without cookies. A successful check-in also sends an anonymous “Check-in <statue>” event, without any location.

Satoshi Fountain giveaway and tips

  • The giveaway runs at selected statues (Budapest, Lugano, El Zonte, Vienna). It only uses data when you take part.
  • A GPS check-in at one of these statues creates a single-use code that is valid for 21 minutes. We store a hash of the code, your hashed device ID, the statue and the time, and delete it after 1 day.
  • If you claim the giveaway we store the Lightning address you enter, the statue, the amount, the payout status and the time. The full address is kept for 90 days for our payout records, then deleted. After that we only keep a masked form (like “sa…@w….com”) and a salted hash so each address can only be used once per statue.
  • Only the masked form is ever shown publicly, in the statue’s recent activity list.
  • Your IP address is used for daily limits as a salted, per-day hash and deleted after 7 days.
  • Tips are sent from your own wallet straight to our Bitcoin tip address (the same one as on the map’s home page). We don’t store anything about tips.
  • To send a payout, our server asks the wallet service behind your Lightning address for an invoice, and our Lightning payment provider pays it.

Deleting your data

Email support@satoshistatues.com with your leaderboard nickname (or the statue and rough time of your check-in) and we will delete your stamps and nickname. Clearing your browser data removes your local passport and device ID.
