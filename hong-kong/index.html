About

Unveiled on 20 Apr 2026 at the Hong Kong Web3 Festival at the Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre, Wan Chai, and donated to the Hong Kong SAR Government. No permanent display site has been reported, so its current location is unknown. The map pin marks the unveiling venue.

📖 The story

Artist
Valentina Picozzi (Satoshigallery).Sources: MicroBit press release
Unveiled
20 Apr 2026, at the Hong Kong Web3 Festival at the Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre, Wan Chai, in a donation ceremony.Sources: MicroBit press release Odaily
Commissioned by
MicroBit Capital Management and HashKey Group, with several early industry figures. It was unveiled by Wilson Fung (MicroBit CEO) and Ryan Ru (HashKey Exchange CEO) and donated to the Hong Kong SAR Government to thank it for its regulatory environment.Sources: MicroBit press release PANews
What the design means
A contemplating figure with a notebook on its lap, nearly invisible from the front, "symbolizing Satoshi Nakamoto's dedication to decentralization, allowing the community to continue his legacy after his retirement". The base is engraved "To Hong Kong — In Math We Trust".Sources: PANews
Materials
304 stainless steel and weathering steel, about 1 cubic metre.Sources: PANews
Series
7th of the series (MicroBit: six had appeared before it).Sources: MicroBit press release
Where it is now
Unknown. No permanent display location has been reported since the donation.Sources: PANews Phemex News

🧭 Visiting

Getting there

Current location unknown, so check before you plan a trip. The statue was unveiled at the Hong Kong Web3 Festival in Hall 5BCDE of the Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre (HKCEC), Wan Chai, and was then donated to the Hong Kong SAR Government.

For the HKCEC itself (only useful if the statue is shown there again): MTR Exhibition Centre station (East Rail line) Exit B3 or Wan Chai station (Island line) Exit A5, about a 10-minute walk, or the Star Ferry to Wan Chai pier (HKCEC).

Exact spot

Unknown. Ownership passed to the Hong Kong SAR Government, and no permanent display location has been published (English and Chinese sources searched, Oct 2026). The map pin marks the unveiling venue, the HKCEC in Wan Chai (OpenStreetMap), not a confirmed current location.

Photo tip

From the side it reads as a figure in deep contemplation with a notebook on its lap; head-on it is almost invisible. The base is engraved "To Hong Kong — In Math We Trust" (PANews).

Hours & access

Unknown. It was unveiled indoors in a convention hall during the festival; where and when it can be seen now has not been published.
