About

Installed at the New York Stock Exchange by Twenty One Capital and announced by the NYSE on 10 Dec 2025. It is indoors and not open to the general public.

🧭 Visiting

Getting there

The New York Stock Exchange is at 11 Wall St; its columned front faces Broad St. By subway, Broad St (J/Z) is closest. From Wall St (4/5), exit at Wall St and Broadway and walk east; from Wall St (2/3), exit at Wall St and William St and walk west (National Park Service; NYC Tourism).

Parking: street parking is extremely limited, and vehicle traffic is restricted around the exchange.

Exact spot

Inside the NYSE building. Bitcoin Austria and Trending Topics place it in the NYSE museum; the exact room is unconfirmed.

Photo tip

You can't photograph the statue itself without an invitation. The public shot is the NYSE façade on Broad Street; a tour-guide site says weekends are the least crowded.

Hours & access

Not publicly accessible. NYSE tours are no longer offered to the general public (they ended after 9/11), and visitors are admitted to the floor only with the Exchange's permission (NYC Tourism; NYSE Rule 37 memo). You can photograph the façade from Broad Street at any time.
