About

Often called the world’s first public Bitcoin monument. Large Corten steel structure featuring the Bitcoin “B” symbol (about 3 tonnes). Located on a roundabout near the city center. Linked to Slovenia’s early crypto scene (supported by Bitstamp).

🧭 Visiting

Getting there

From OpenStreetMap (straight-line distances; routes and timetables unverified): Kranj bus station (Avtobusna postaja Kranj) is about 260 m away, and Kranj railway station (Železniška postaja Kranj) about 1.1 km.

Parking: OpenStreetMap shows a paid surface car park about 30 m from the roundabout.

Exact spot

In the roundabout in front of the Kranj courthouse (RTV Slovenija). OpenStreetMap names it "Bitcoin krožišče" (Bitcoin roundabout), and the District Court (Okrožno sodišče v Kranju) is about 40 m away. Pin: 46.24636, 14.35781 (OpenStreetMap node 5903169142).

Photo tip

This isn't a see-through design. RTV Slovenija notes that the monument can best be seen from the air.

Generic tip (not from a source): shoot diagonally from a sidewalk corner so the tilted ring and the B symbol both read, and wait for a gap in traffic.

Hours & access

It sits in an active traffic circle, so view it from the surrounding sidewalks and don't walk into the roundabout. As a street monument it has no opening hours.
