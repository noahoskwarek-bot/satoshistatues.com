Timeline of Satoshi statues

Oldest first. Planned and not-yet-public entries are labelled. Each line links to that statue’s page.

  1. 13 Mar 2018
    Kranj Bitcoin Monument
    Kranj, Slovenia · Installed

    Often called the world’s first public Bitcoin monument: a large Corten-steel “B” in a city roundabout.

  2. 16 Sep 2021
    Satoshi Statue Budapest
    Budapest, Hungary · Installed

    World’s first Satoshi statue, with a reflective face so visitors see themselves (“We are all Satoshi”).

  3. 6–9 Apr 2022
    Miami Bitcoin Bull
    Miami, USA · Installed

    Chrome-and-fiberglass robotic bull with blue laser eyes, first shown at Bitcoin 2022 then moved to Miami Dade College.

  4. 25 Oct 2024
    Satoshi Statue Lugano
    Lugano, Switzerland · Back on display (Oct 2026)

    First of Valentina Picozzi’s optical-illusion Satoshi statues, unveiled for Plan ₿ Forum (later restored after a move).

  5. ~1 Feb 2025 (San Salvador); Apr 2025 (El Zonte)
    Satoshi Statue El Zonte
    El Zonte, El Salvador · Installed

    21 Nodes statue that moved from Museo MARTE in San Salvador to Bitcoin Beach’s new public space.

  6. 25 Apr 2025
    Satoshi Statue Tokyo
    Tokyo, Japan · Installed

    21 Nodes statue outside Tokyo Bitcoin Base, unveiled at the Base’s grand opening.

  7. 1 May 2025
    Satoshi Statue Fornelli
    Fornelli, Italy · Installed

    Italy’s first Bitcoin/Satoshi monument, in a Molise village known for high Bitcoin merchant adoption.

  8. 9 Sep 2025
    Satoshi Statue Hanoi
    Hanoi, Vietnam · Installed

    21 Nodes statue indoors in the Blockchain Gallery at 1Matrix headquarters in Times City.

  9. 11 Nov 2025
    Satoshi Statue Miami (Valentina Picozzi)
    Miami, USA · Planned

    21 Nodes statue unveiled in Miami Beach; a permanent waterfront park site was announced but not confirmed.

  10. 10 Dec 2025
    Satoshi Statue New York
    New York, USA · Installed

    21 Nodes statue installed indoors at the New York Stock Exchange; not open to the general public.

  11. 1 Apr 2026
    Satoshi Statue Hòa Lạc
    Hòa Lạc, Vietnam · Installed Apr 2026 · on a university campus

    A 2 m stainless-steel “vanishing” Satoshi by Valentina Picozzi on Blockchain Hill at FPT University’s Hòa Lạc campus.

  12. 20 Apr 2026
    Satoshi Statue Hong Kong
    Hong Kong, China · Unveiled & donated (Apr 2026), no permanent site reported

    21 Nodes statue unveiled at the Hong Kong Web3 Festival and given to the Hong Kong SAR Government; no permanent public site reported.

  13. 14 Oct 2026
    Satoshi Statue Vienna
    Vienna, Austria · Installed

    21 Nodes statue unveiled by Bitcoin Austria for its 15th anniversary, outdoors at Viertel Zwei.

  14. 29 Oct 2026
    Satoshi Monument Buenos Aires
    Buenos Aires, Argentina · Installed Oct 2026 · outdoors, best on weekdays

    “El Génesis de Satoshi Nakamoto”: a 2.4 m stainless-steel and glass cube inspired by Bitcoin’s genesis block, at Polo DOT.
