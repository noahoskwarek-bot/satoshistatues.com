🛂 Satoshi Passport

    To collect a stamp, open a statue on the map and tap Check in while you're within about 200 m of it. Stamps are kept only in this browser on this device. Nothing is uploaded, and clearing your browser data erases them. Statues that aren't installed yet can't be stamped.
