21 Nodes: Valentina Picozzi’s Satoshi statues

“21 Nodes” is the Satoshi Gallery project by Italian artist Valentina Picozzi to place 21 identical “disappearing” Satoshi Nakamoto statues around the world, one for each million of Bitcoin’s 21 million coin cap. [Bitcoin Austria] [TokenPost]

About the count

Bitcoin Austria’s 21 Nodes page lists six nodes: New York, El Zonte, Lugano, Vienna, Hanoi and Tokyo [source]. Miami (unveiled November 2025 as the series’ fifth statue [source]) and Hong Kong (a Valentina Picozzi statue unveiled in April 2026, described as the next of the same limited edition after Switzerland, El Zonte, Tokyo, Hanoi, Miami and the NYSE [source]) are included from press reports. We count a node as installed once it stands at a public site, so Miami is not counted yet. Sources disagree on the numbering of the early statues. Budapest (2021), Fornelli and the other monuments on the map are separate works and not part of 21 Nodes.
