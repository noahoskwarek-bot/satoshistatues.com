About

First of the optical-illusion series. Unveiled in Parco Ciani in Oct 2024, it was later moved to the Foce, the mouth of the Cassarate river, where vandals threw it into Lake Lugano on the night of 1–2 Aug 2025; divers recovered it in pieces. The artist repaired it with the kintsugi technique and it was re-inaugurated on 24 Oct 2025 in the central area of Parco Ciani, near the Dying Socrates sculpture. On 19 Apr 2026 it was vandalized again and found seriously damaged in its central body. An OpenStreetMap mapper noted it was no longer at its originally mapped spot as of 28 Jul 2026; current status unconfirmed.

What the design means
It is designed to disappear as you change your viewpoint, "symbolizing Satoshi's retreat from the public eye after laying the foundation of a revolutionary financial system". It is the first statue of the 21 Nodes series.Sources: Tether / Plan ₿ (Oct 2024) Bitcoin Austria: Satoshi statue page
History
After the unveiling it was moved to the Foce, at the mouth of the Cassarate river. Around 2 a.m. on Saturday 2 Aug 2025 a group of youths threw it into the lake; divers recovered it on 3 Aug. It was repaired and re-inaugurated on 24 Oct 2025 after a petition with about 1,300 signatures led by Luca Esposito (Satoshi Spritz). It was vandalized again on 19 Apr 2026.Sources: RSI (Aug 2025) Corriere del Ticino (24 Oct 2025) RSI (19 Apr 2026)

🧭 Visiting

Getting there

Check that the statue is actually on display before you go (see Hours & access).

From OpenStreetMap (straight-line distances from the 2024 statue position; routes unverified): the Palazzo Congressi bus stop is about 130 m away, and the Lugano Centro bus hub (TPL) about 360 m.

Parking: the paid underground Autosilo Piazza Castello is about 135 m away (OpenStreetMap).

Exact spot

In Parco Ciani, Lugano's lakefront park. The statue was first installed there in Oct 2024 (pin 46.00441, 8.95659, OpenStreetMap node 12288711744), then moved to the Foce (Cassarate river mouth), where it was thrown into the lake on 1–2 Aug 2025. On 24 Oct 2025 it was re-inaugurated in the central area of Parco Ciani, near the "Dying Socrates" sculpture (Corriere del Ticino; Il Giornale del Ticino). That exact spot may differ from the 2024 pin and is unverified.

Photo tip

It's a Satoshigallery design, so the figure reads from the side and nearly vanishes from the front. After the April 2026 damage, its current look is unknown.

Generic tip (not from a source): if it's on display, the lake makes a clean background for a head-on shot.

Hours & access

Current status unconfirmed. It was found seriously damaged on 19 Apr 2026 (RSI; Ticinonline), and an OpenStreetMap mapper noted it was no longer there as of 28 Jul 2026. Parco Ciani itself is open daily from 6:30 to 23:30 (March–October) and 6:30 to 21:00 (November–February), per the City of Lugano.
