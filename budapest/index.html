About

World’s first Satoshi statue. Reflective face so visitors see themselves (“We are all Satoshi”). Free public access 24/7.

📖 The story

Artists
Réka Gergely and Tamás Gilly, Hungarian sculptors.Sources: Statue of Satoshi (official site)
Unveiled
16 Sep 2021, in Graphisoft Park, Budapest.Sources: Statue of Satoshi (official site)
Commissioned by
Founders from the Hungarian crypto community: András Györfi (Kripto Akadémia, who came up with the idea), Gabi Debreczeni-Raskó and Barnabás Debreczeni (MrCoin), Imre Szilágyi (Blockchain Budapest) and Kornél Kalocsai (Blockchain Hungary Association). It was community-funded.Sources: Statue of Satoshi (official site)
What the design means
A generic human figure in a hoodie with the Bitcoin logo on the chest, because Satoshi's gender, race, age and height are unknown. The face is reflective, so every visitor sees themselves in it: "We are all Satoshi".Sources: Statue of Satoshi (official site)
Materials
Bronze, with a face of bronze-aluminium composite.Sources: Statue of Satoshi (official site)
Notable
Widely described as the world's first statue of Satoshi Nakamoto.Sources: Statue of Satoshi (official site) Trending Topics

🧭 Visiting

Getting there

From OpenStreetMap (straight-line distances; lines and timetables unverified): the nearest bus stops are Sujtás utca (about 400 m) and Záhony utca (about 470 m). The nearest rail stops are the Aquincum railway halt (about 780 m) and the Kaszásdűlő HÉV suburban-rail stop (about 800 m).

Parking: OpenStreetMap shows a 120-space surface lot about 180 m away. Whether it's public or for park tenants only is unverified.

Exact spot

In Graphisoft Park, Záhony u. 7, 1031 Budapest (District III), per the official site. Köztérkép, the Hungarian public-art register, places it on the green area in front of the Microsoft building, facing the Heracles (Herkules) statue. In OpenStreetMap, the Herkules statue is about 15 m away and the Microsoft office about 40 m. Pin: 47.56072, 19.05438 (OpenStreetMap node 9843217531).

It is not in City Park (Városliget), despite what some listings say.

Photo tip

This isn't a see-through design. The face is a polished bronze-aluminium composite, so every visitor can see their own face in it (official site).

Generic tip (not from a source): shoot close so your reflection sits inside the hood, and keep the sun behind you to avoid flare.

Hours & access

Outdoors. The official site says it can be visited 24/7, free of charge.
